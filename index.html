From lost to returned in three steps

No group chats to scroll. Everything about an item lives on one card.

Post the item

Choose Lost or Found, add a photo, the place and the date. Set a 4-digit PIN so only you can close the post later.

Search and match

Use the Lost and Found tabs, search by name or place, and filter by category until you spot yours.

Contact and hand over

Send a private claim and answer the check question. The poster reviews claims on their report and marks it returned with their PIN.

Browse items

Open any card to see full details and contact the person who posted it.

Report an item

It takes about a minute. Your email is stored privately for claim follow-up. No email is sent automatically.
